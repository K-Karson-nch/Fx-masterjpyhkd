How it works

Three numbers. No surprises.

The estimate is deliberately simple: the Mastercard rate for your direction, your card's fee, and the amount you plan to spend.

01

Choose the direction

Mastercard prices JPY → HKD and HKD → JPY separately. The converter uses the exact rate for the direction shown, not a mathematical inverse.

02

Add your card fee

Enter the foreign transaction fee from your card terms. It is applied to the converted amount, so the estimate reflects what may reach your statement.

03

See the estimate

Your issuer may use a different settlement rate or add its own charges. Treat the total as a useful estimate, not a guaranteed final charge.

Rate data

Straight from Mastercard's public converter.

A GitHub Action checks Mastercard every 3 hours and updates the small rate file this page reads. The page checks for a newer file when it opens and every 15 minutes. The site itself is static HTML, so it loads quickly without a server or tracker.
